Founded on 23rd September 2020 by Mr. Govind Shukla (Founder & CEO), InAmigos Foundation is a registered non-profit based in Chhattisgarh. With certifications like ISO 9001:2015 and NITI Aayog registration, we bring transparency and quality to every initiative.

✨ Our Ongoing Projects ✨

Project Seva

Providing nutritious meals, clothing, and essential supplies to underprivileged families across urban & rural clusters. We believe no one sleeps hungry.

🎯 Impact: 2,00,000+ meals served

BachpanSala

Nurturing young minds through quality education, digital learning support, after-school mentoring, and school kits for children who lack access.

🎯 1500+ children educated & enrolled

Project Jeev

Animal rescue, sterilization, ambulance support, and feeding stray animals. We protect the voiceless with medical camps and shelter coordination.

🐶 3000+ animals rescued & treated

Project Udaan

Women empowerment through tailoring, financial literacy, and entrepreneurship workshops. Creating income-generating opportunities & self-reliance.

🌸 700+ women trained, 200+ self-employed

Project Prakriti

Tree plantations, river clean-up drives, plastic waste awareness, and sustainable farming support. "Plant for a better tomorrow".

🌿 35,000+ trees planted | 50+ campaigns

Project Vikas

Skill development & vocational training (IT, hospitality, handicrafts) to enhance employability among youth from marginalized communities.

📈 1200+ youth skilled, 60% job placements

💖 Creating a Ripple of Compassion

Our purpose goes beyond immediate relief — we work for sustainable change. By integrating education, environmental consciousness, animal welfare, and women empowerment, we build resilient communities.
